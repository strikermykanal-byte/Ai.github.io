The New Reality

Today, artificial intelligence has increased its role in human life. If you think about it, you can understand how significant an impact AI has in our time: creating websites, various web applications, mobile applications, and even games — all of this can already be done by a neural network with just a dozen prompts. But, of course, not everything is as great as it’s written online. Most of these tasks are within the capabilities of various neural networks, but only paid ones. If you’re an ordinary user or a beginner developer of various types of content, you’ll most likely be using a free subscription. However, the free version of AI also has quite good creative capabilities. There are many pros and cons to using them, but let’s look at the pros: 1. Effectiveness in various tasks when used in a balanced way. If you approach neural networks in the right way, you can really gain a decent amount of knowledge. For example, you might be a beginner in the field of web development. I, as the author of this website, am a junior developer myself — though judging by knowledge rather than experience, I’m already ready to move on to commercial offers. My journey started with projects with 8-9 lines of code and were ridiculous. Now, as you can see, I'm getting good multi-page websites with a good style.| I'm still struggling with deceptive thoughts like, "You're using AI to develop your website again. Don't you know how to do it? It's basic logic." That's the problem. After all, we are trying from the very beginning to use our little knowledge in development, even without AI. Tip: don’t be afraid to use artificial intelligence, no matter what level of programmer you are. Experienced workers have known this for a long time and don’t worry about it at all, but for an inexperienced developer, it’s always difficult. 2. Acquiring new information and overall development. No matter what anyone says, a lot has changed with the advent of AI. Humanity is moving forward and doesn’t want to stop. Medicine and many other sciences have begun to gain momentum in their development. It has become easier for people to acquire information in general, without having to struggle with finding and fixing errors in various tasks. It has become much easier to learn the language you need, to engage in IT development, and MUCH more. 3. Automation. This point is superficially related to the second point. But it touches on a slightly different topic. Nowadays, large companies won’t have to create projects with hundreds, or even thousands, of lines of code. This also works with scientific research — instead of lengthy calculations, the AI will explain and provide examples for a given task. If you take these points into account and stick to only these points, then artificial intelligence is the ideal option for various issues or for creating the necessary programs. The main thing is not to cross the line between conscious use and the use of the four keys “Ctrl +C, Ctrl +V”.

What about the drawbacks of AI?

Now, I think it’s worth moving on to the downsides. Perhaps, as you read this information, you’ll see your everyday self here. 1. Wibcoding. The first point will be the most acute and challenging to start with, but it’s also a term that’s quite common in our lives. A “wibcoder” is a person (a developer or someone responsible for a specific task) who, instead of using AI wisely, simply uses the key combinations I mentioned in the Pros category (Ctrl+C, Ctrl+V). As I said, neural networks are already capable of creating a game based on a precise description of what is desired. But people have forgotten that it needs to be used wisely, not just taken from AI. The downside of these vibe coders is that most of them don’t even know how to create a block within which certain elements will be defined and with which you can interact during the process. This is just an example from web development, and there are plenty of such developments nowadays.2. Employers’ requirements for workers. This is also one of the challenging nuances for developers in 2026. In addition to the development of AI, there is fierce competition among junior developers. The programming market has exceeded all possible figures. The consequences of this are also unpleasant, ranging from the fact that there are so many newcomers like you to the developed thinking of AI. 3. Self‑deception about the unlimited potential of AI. I believe this is a global deception perpetrated by the creators of artificial intelligence. Over time, we’ve been led to believe that AI is a tool that literally knows EVERYTHING. Although, if you have even the slightest logical thinking ability, you can understand that it has a certain database, based on which it selects words tailored to a specific user. There aren’t many neural networks that even exist that have the ability to check how up‑to‑date the information taken from the internet is and sort it. This is the main problem — companies immediately inform users not to trust AI 100 %, since it has a lot of information but cannot interact with specific and necessary data.. 4. Brain rot. By brain rot, I mean people who use only AI to find new information or clarify old information. The thing is that, as I said earlier, AI has a certain set of data, but it cannot sort it by relevance. And the person who doesn’t just look for information on Google or another search engine and doesn’t verify it eventually loses the ability to think for themselves. You can check this yourself by first going to a neural network and asking any question, and then asking the same question on the internet. You can immediately feel the difference.

Conclusions

Well, I think it’s worth drawing a brief conclusion on this topic. In our time, neural networks are very highly developed. This fact can be both encouraging and alarming. After all, quite a few people have lost their jobs due to the huge competition and the development of AI. Following the development of neural networks, there has been enormous competition among aspiring developers. If you use a neural network with intelligence and a specific task in mind, it will be a fairly good option. But if you decide to simply copy text, then congratulations — you’ll start to degrade after some time.
